SokoHub Marketplace

Empowering Local Businesses Through Digital Trade

SokoHub is a modern online marketplace created to uplift and empower small and medium-sized local businesses. We provide a simple, accessible, and reliable platform where vendors can showcase their products, reach new customers, and grow beyond traditional retail limits.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between physical shops and digital commerce, making it easier for entrepreneurs to take their businesses online—even with little technical experience.

We Believe In:

  • ✔ Supporting local businesses with digital tools
  • ✔ Creating equal opportunities in the online economy
  • ✔ Building trust between sellers and buyers
  • ✔ Encouraging innovation in African e-commerce

Whether you’re a vendor looking to expand your reach or a customer searching for quality local products, SokoHub is your marketplace—built for growth, reliability, and convenience.
